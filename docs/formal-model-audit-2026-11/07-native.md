## Unmodeled state machines (first pass)


1. **One-live-MainActivity hand-off** (`MainActivity.java:128-204,223-239,337-364`). State: `liveInstance`, `liveTaskId/liveTaskHome`, `unackedFrontStarts`, `handOffUnreliable`, `handedOff`. Events: HOME, launcher, notification and front-marker starts, relaunch, destroy, resume. Invariants at risk: one page owner, no finish/restart HOME loop, intent delivered exactly once.
2. **`ListeningService` convergence** (`ListeningService.java:163-285`). `refresh` → start/stop service → `apply`, capture thread, three timers, recogniser executor, epoch tickets. At risk: no frame analysed without lease and epoch; every `startForegroundService` followed by `startForeground`.
3. **`SpeechGate`** (`SpeechGate.java:53-106`). Energy VAD with preroll, hangover, max length and adaptive floor. Pure Java.
4. **`NativeLiveVoicePipe`** (`NativeLiveVoicePipe.java:17-103`). `closing/cancelled/finished/cutoff`, eight-batch queue, `ready/closed` futures. At risk: no post after cancel; `closed` always completes.
5. **`AlphaLiveVoiceCapture` + `LiveCaptureResource`**. Start/attach/cancel/stop/interrupt against the worker loop; exactly one `ended`.
6. **Resident start/attach/cancel/credential-shutdown-retry** (`AlphaLocalAgentPlugin.java:117-146,460-587,592-610`). Eight interacting statics plus `cancelledStarts`. `ResidentRuntime` covers only an abstraction.
7. **`AlphaNavigationService`** static session and service instance (75-105,122-191). Only projected via `NavigationProgress`; queued START vs STOP ordering is not modelled.
8. **Multi-recipient SMS** (`AlphaSms.java:286-305`). Partial submission then throw; journal prune → tombstone → Bloom compaction.
9. **`NativeDeletionCoordinator`** multi-row reconcile (129-156), unique-work `REPLACE` (158), the 5 s `visible` owner cache (73-80), and reminder deletions.
10. **`AlphaAssistActivity` invocation** (`invocation/invocationAt/invocationLocked`, single-use `takeInvocation`) and `AssistInvocation.take`.
11. **`AlphaVoiceCloudPlugin` playback** (file vs stream vs preparing, focus, drain barrier, permission epochs); `CloudSignInFlow` single-use attempt; `WorkflowNoticeIntentDedup`. These were surveyed, not read in full.
12. **Boot/alarm rescheduling.** `ReminderReceiver` delegates to upstream `ReminderEngine` (outside this slice). The notification-listener connection (`AlphaNotificationListener`) is likewise upstream.

## Modeling opportunities (first pass)


1. **`HomeInstanceHandoff.qnt`** for B1. Invariants: at most one page-owning instance; hand-off never loops (bounded by `MAX_UNACKED_FRONT_STARTS`); task kind is fixed per task id across relaunch.
2. **Extend `AlwaysOnListener`** with per-reason pauses, latch-during-pause and `sleepLapse`. Extract `Listener.observeIndicatorLease` into a pure class and drive it from ITF traces.
3. **`ListenerServiceLifecycle.qnt`** for refresh/start/stop/apply ordering. Invariant: no stop while a foreground start is outstanding. A canary for C2.
4. **`RendererRecovery` with two activities** on one renderer. Invariant: one renderer death spends one loss.
5. **`LiveCapturePermission` scenario** in `SpeechResources`: pause during a pending permission, call during opening, and real exclusivity between `capture` and `live`.
6. **`ResidentScheduling`**: replace `finishLifecycle` with explicit `start()`; add `cancelStart` and shutdown retry.
7. **`ResetRecovery`**: split `authenticate` into record → bounded revocation → dispatch, with "marker present but directory fsync failed" and cross-process failure-file writes.
8. **ITF-drivable pure Java today:**
   - `SpeechGate`
   - `IndicatorLease` and `CrashLoopBreaker` (currently hand-written assertions only)
   - `ListeningEpoch`
   - `LiveCaptureAdmission` and `LiveCaptureResource`
   - `SpeechPcmQueue`
   - `StreamingSpeechPlayback` with a fake `Output` (against `StreamingPlayback`)
   - `NativeVoiceIpc.exchange` with a fake `Port` (against `VoiceIpcAssembly`)
   - `NativeLiveVoicePipe`
   - `DeferredDeletionJournal` with an in-memory `Store` (against `DeletionJournal`)
   - `SmsSendJournal` (against `DeviceActions`)
   - `ResidentScheduleRearm.decide`, the full decision table from `ResidentScheduling` traces
   - `ResetAuthorization` and `AlphaDataReset.automaticAttemptAllowed` (against `ResetRecovery`)
   - `AssistInvocation`, `ResidentCredentialShutdownRetry`, `ResidentRecoveryReview`
