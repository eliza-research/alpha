## Unmodeled state machines (first pass)


Of my five AlphaView values, **phone, messages, contacts and apps have no production-behaviour model**; **settings** has only the historical `legacy/SettingsNav.qnt`. Phone has no in-app production view at all (`P/mvp-features.ts:11-18`; `R/aosp-image.ts` admits only `messages`). Apps is a shell drawer flag, not a registered view.

1. **Messages / SMS send** (`P/sms-adapter.ts:11-22`, `R/image-sms.ts`). State: `active`, `lifecycle`, `draftRevision`, `operationId`, `busy`, and receipt status across seven values. Session: `epoch`, `visit`, `observing`, `stop`, `thread`, `next`. It drives real sends with idempotency, so "never send twice" and "stale result never shown" are safety properties.
2. **`SmsSendState` is dead code.** `advanceSmsState`, `smsMayBeReviewedAgain` and `reviewSms` (`R/aosp-telephony.ts:159-198`) have no caller under `apps/app/src`; the adapter validates ad hoc (`sms-adapter.ts:18`).
3. **Contacts** (`P/contacts-adapter.ts:9-33`): `active`, `epoch`, `loading`, `readable`, `more`, `limit`, plus the permission request.
4. **View admission.** `ENABLED_MVP_VIEWS`/`DEFERRED_MVP_VIEWS` are mutated at runtime together with `ORDER` and the `S()` mask (`mvp-features.ts:43`, `apps/app/src/main.tsx:303`, `model.js:5211`). It guards image-only surfaces.
5. **Sign-in renderer controller.** `SignInView` step × `signInPending` × `operation` × `queuedSignInReturn` (single slot) × `methodsRefresh` × `inAppMethodsUnconfirmed` (`R/connection-ui.tsx:138-143`, 563-580, 705-706, 2006-2021). IdentitySession covers native only.
6. **Readiness probe cache** (`AlphaCloudSignInPlugin.java:190-216`): per-environment `probing` coalescing, TTLs, and forget-on-refusal.
7. **EntitlementRefresher internals**: `fallbackActive`, `lapsedPlanName`, backoff and terminal states (`subscription-entitlement.ts:368-455`), plus `lastPlanRecheck`/`planManagementReturn` throttles (`connection-ui.tsx:2204-2221`).
8. **Settings async state** (`P/settings-adapter.ts:175-186`): `generation`, `scaleGeneration`, `listenerSequence`, `capabilityAbort`, the busy flags, and `licenses.status`. Sub-navigation (`page`/`acct`/`adding`/`sheet`/`log`) and `actionHistoryRead` (`P/native-adapter.ts:26`, 358-395) are also unmodeled.
9. **Cloud delegation receiver.** Native capture (`J/AlphaConnectionPlugin.java:90-99`) and the `Pending` compare-exchange machine with start/status/complete/cancel/revoke (`R/cloud-delegation-ui.tsx:50-110`).
10. **Development transport proposals** (`R/development-transport.ts:113-212`): `pending` cleared per send, consumed before dispatch.

## Modeling opportunities (first pass)


**New Quint modules or invariants**
- **IdentitySession additions**: an `authorizing` phase; Google→MFA; per-attempt expiry; `transportFailure` in `code`/`mfa`; `resendFailed`. Add a renderer mirror variable with invariant `uiStep ∈ {code, mfa} ⇒ nativeAttempt == uiAttempt` (would have caught C1, C2). Add `cooldown ⇒ a send actually left` (C3) and `oldKey ⇒ eventually revoked or reported` (C5).
- **SmsSend**: operationId × journal status × draftRevision × lifecycle. Invariants: at most one dispatch per operationId; `cancelled`/`denied` permits a new operationId without an edit (C4); stale results never rendered.
- **ViewAdmission**: image × adapter-admitted × raw view × masked view. Invariant: raw view is never a deferred view after revoke (C7).
- **SignInChooser**: pending-hold/auto-resume, the methods-refresh single-flight, and the queued-return slot.
- **EntitlementRefresher refinement**: a clock-derived view so `ExpiryHonest` holds, and a retained-on-invalid branch.
- **GoogleConnect runtime** (replacing the legacy comparison): four statuses, a three-valued return, owner epoch.
- **DelegationReceiver** composed with CallbackLanding.
- **ContactsSession**: epoch/loading liveness (loading is eventually false).

**Pure functions for pre/postcondition verification**
- `managedServiceRequest`, `googleCalendarEvents` (`R/managed-service-routes.ts`): result true ⇒ origin is production API, no `%`, `\` or `//` in the path, and the method/body/query shape matches the table.
- `resolveCloudEnvironment`: `staging` ⇒ `testMocks` or the dev non-Android cloud-resident case.
- `nextRefreshDelay`: result is null or within `[1, REFRESH_RETRY_MAX_MS]` when failures > 0, and never 0.
- `planAdmission`, `subscriptionView`: totality; `inactive` ⇒ `needs-plan`.
- `gmailGrantChange`, `gmailGrantTier`, `gmailActionGrant`, `gmailReadCacheKey` (`R/gmail-grants.ts`): modify ⇒ readable; `dropReadCache` ⇒ every held modify capability is in `withdrawn`.
- `signInReadiness`, `normalizeSignInCode`, `authenticated`, `googleOutcome` (`R/cloud-sign-in.ts`): no extra keys accepted; output environment equals input.
- `reviewSms`, `advanceSmsState`, `normalizePhoneNumber`, `parseSmsSendResult` (`R/aosp-telephony.ts`): recipients deduplicated; terminal states have no successors; `delivered` ⇒ all parts success.
- `detectAospImage`, `admittedAospViews`, `deferredMvpPrompt`: non-android ⇒ false; result ⊆ `AOSP_IMAGE_VIEWS`.
- `googleAuthorizationUrlAllowed` (`R/google-authorization.ts`): scopes ⊆ allowlist; callback origin in the fixed set.
- `connectionChooserVisible`, `productionCloudSignInRequired` (`connection-ui.tsx:2490-2500`).
- `handoffGate`, `tileFactsFromSnapshot` (`P/native-adapter.ts`).
- Java: `CloudSignInFlow.parseCallback` (SUCCESS only from fragment; RETURN never carries a code), `Pending.serialize`/`parse` round-trip, `failureForStatus`, `ackBackoffMs`, `GoogleConnectReturn.parse`.
