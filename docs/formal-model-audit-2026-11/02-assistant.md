## Unmodeled state machines (first pass)


1. **Composer send attempt** (`agent-adapter.ts:63-83,1181-1264`). Flags `transportCalled/preDispatch/streamed/settled`, a 5 s settle race, and the `dispatched()` decision between restoring text and offering "Check for reply". Invariant: text is restored iff not dispatched; never resent.
2. **Recorder/dictation** (`S/prototype/voice-adapter.ts:113-300`). `stage` closed/ready/starting/recording/recorded/transcribing/review, plus `generation`, `busy`, the `stopping` promise chain, `retirement`, `startingCapture`. No manifest lists this file.
3. **Voice-conversation host** (`voice-adapter.ts:402-436,486-616`). `chatDestination`, `ownedNavigation` (`applying`, `context`), `conversationPrepared`, `conversationReview`, three engines.
4. **Media-cleanup latches.** `mediaUnconfirmed` is set at 8 sites and never cleared (`voice-adapter.ts:121,176,198,392,455,561,610,640,651`). Parallel latches: `speechRetirement` (`S/prototype/local-speech-playback.ts:15-20`), `retirement` (`S/runtime/app-speech.ts:6,22`), `appSpeechRetirement` (`S/runtime/cloud-voice.ts:87,89,103`).
5. **`speakChunk`** (`S/local-speech-playback.ts:15-41`). Listeners, prepare with 100 ms busy retry, play with busy retry, started, ended/failed/interrupted, dispose (strict vs 500 ms bounded).
6. **Read-aloud and note reading** (`S/prototype/local-speech-playback.ts:24,38,98-134`), and `voiceTurn` follow-up (`:64,135-157`).
7. **Live ASR session** (`S/runtime/live-voice.ts:95-215`): current turn, `lastSeq`, `finalized`, event/response failure dedup counters. **Segment stream** (`S/runtime/live-segment-stream.ts:29-93`): `open/cut/finished/discarded/expected`. **Resident capture** (`S/runtime/native-resident-capture.ts:16-71`): `active/stopping/discarded/expected/pending≤8/finished≤512`. Invariant: exactly one result per closed segment.
8. **Native dictation recording** (`S/runtime/native-live-voice.ts:201-273`): opening, recording, stopping, clip/lost/cancelled, max-duration timer.
9. **Native SSE bridge** (`S/runtime/local-agent-native-stream.ts:12-72`): `active/started/responded/terminal`, 120 s bound.
10. **Stopped-reply reconciliation** (`S/runtime/connection-ui.tsx:1233-1262`, `agent-adapter.ts:321-342`); **resident auto-retry** 3/10/30/60 s ×4 (`connection-ui.tsx:626-632`); **saved-sign-in reconnect** 4 s→60 s (`:2536-2541`).
11. **Read-reply leases** `pending/completing/done/cancelled/unconfirmed` (`agent-adapter.ts:220-256`); **pending-action recovery** key/failedKey/expiry timer (`:257-318`).
12. **Heard reporter** (`S/runtime/voice-turn-routes.ts:80-123`); **voice-timing mark order** (`S/runtime/voice-timing.ts:83-97`); **notice-question** `canceled/composing/ended` (`S/prototype/own-notice-question.ts:8-17`).

## Modeling opportunities (first pass)


**Quint**
- **ComposerSend**, replacing or re-sourcing DraftDispatch: SendAttempt, session switch, overlapping sends. Invariants: `typing` implies an owning live attempt; restored XOR dispatched; explicit-argument sends reach the transport (catches C1 and C3).
- **VoiceSession additions:** streamed pre-reply playback, `opening` vs `capture`, the `awaiting` latch, `starting`/`error`. Invariants: `awaiting-user-input` implies no capture and no opening; speaking implies a non-empty echo reference; every played sentence is reported on interrupt.
- **SegmentAccounting** over the segment stream, resident capture and `segments[]`: one result per closed segment, including on close failure.
- **SpeechCleanupLatch:** one shared model for the four chains, with an explicit recovery or announcement requirement.
- **RendererSpeechChunk** (`speakChunk`): one terminal outcome; dispose always runs; no listener outlives it.
- **StoppedReplyReconciliation** and **ApprovalRoutes** (AlphaClient plus recovered path) with a cross-route OneDispatch.
- **RecorderStage** for the voice-adapter generation/stage machine.

**Function-level TypeScript candidates**
- `splitSpokenSentences` (`voice-assistant.ts:180`): `join(' ')` equals the whitespace-collapsed input; prefix-stability under appended text.
- `planCloudSpeechChunks` (`S/runtime/cloud-speech-chunks.ts:22`): concatenation preserves text; each chunk ≤5000, first ≤220 unless a single word.
- `replaceAssistantDraft`/`readAssistantDraft` (`S/runtime/assistant-draft-record.ts:4,10`): compare-and-swap linearity; a new revision iff the text changed.
- `findStoppedUserTurn` (`S/runtime/stopped-reply-match.ts:15`): id-only matching whenever any id is present.
- `dispatched`/`isPreDispatchError` (`agent-adapter.ts:69-70`): total decision table.
- `readEndOfTurn` (`voice-turn-routes.ts:18`): never `finished` unless `available===true && complete===true`.
- `markVoiceTiming` ordering (`voice-timing.ts:83`): monotonic marks.
- `parseVoiceTranscriptEnvelope`/`parseLiveFinal` (`live-voice.ts:57,71`).
- `anyAbortSignal` (`S/runtime/abort-signal.ts:2`): first-abort reason; listener cleanup.
- `copyContext` + `setViewContext` equality (`alpha-client.ts:154,241`): revision bumps iff the envelope changed.
- `validate`/`choice` (`S/runtime/conversation-selection.ts:7,17`).
- `preparedNavigation` (`local-agent-stream.ts:6`).
