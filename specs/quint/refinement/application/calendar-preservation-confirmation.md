# Mounted Calendar preservation confirmation

`CalendarPreservationConfirmation` covers the Settings Calendar review,
its exact app-record port and inventory fingerprint, and the pending
owner-keyed retention marker. Back, document hiding, pagehide and
connection replacement retire the initiating attempt. The source checks
the attempt before dispatch, lets an already-dispatched IndexedDB
transaction settle, and fences late refreshes and notices. A fresh
current review may reuse an exact committed marker.

The mounted Chromium campaign observes all 14 state fields across 10
schedules and 69 checkpoints. It uses the actual Alpha Settings button,
Calendar controller and browser document store. The schedules include a
same-account session replacement, a newer owner attempt, Back and reentry,
a committed marker, a conflicting CAS, changed legacy bytes before and
after capture, document hiding, visible pagehide and storage retry.
Legacy bytes remain intact. The synthetic app-record transport accepts
only the closed read routes.

The model bounds two owners, four port lifetimes and attempts, and two
inventory fingerprints. The source checkpoints group selected transitions
around stable mounted commits. Broader connection and provider behavior,
arbitrary storage failures, and installed process death are separate.
